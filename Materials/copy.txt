How The Growth Jar Experience works

01. REFLECT
Pause once a week. Notice the moments, people, experiences, and feelings that
shaped your days.
02. WRITE
Capture your gratitude, little wins, meaningful memories, worries, and personal
affirmations using guided prompts.
03. TEAR
Carefully tear out your completed reflection slip. A little piece of your story, now in
your hands.
04. SAVE
Fold your slip and place it in your Growth Jar. Watch your collection grow, one week
at a time.
05. REVISIT
After 52 weeks, open your jar. Read your words, relive your memories, and discover
how much has changed.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to write every day?
No. It's a weekly experience designed to fit into your life. Simply set aside time once
a week.
How is this different from a regular journal?
You don't just write and move on. You tear out a reflection slip each week and
preserve it in a jar, creating a collection of memories to revisit after 52 weeks.
What if I have a difficult week?
You can write about that, too. Gratitude doesn't mean pretending everything is
perfect. There's room for your worries, emotions, and lessons.
What happens after 52 weeks?
You'll open your jar, read your saved reflections, and use the journal's final reflection
section to look back on your year.
Does the journal come with a jar?
Details of the journal-only and complete experience packages will be shared at
launch.
Can I gift it to someone?
Absolutely. It's a thoughtful gift for birthdays, milestones, new beginnings, or
anyone who wants to become more intentional about their life.
